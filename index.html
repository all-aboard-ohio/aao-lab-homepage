About the Program

The All Aboard Ohio Dev Program is a community-driven initiative dedicated to growing Ohio's developer ecosystem. We connect aspiring and experienced developers with mentors, resources, and real-world projects — helping talent flourish right here in the Buckeye State.

🎓

Learn

Access curated workshops, coding challenges, and self-paced learning paths designed for all skill levels.

🤝

Connect

Meet fellow developers, mentors, and Ohio tech employers at our regular meetups and virtual events.

🚀

Build

Contribute to open-source projects, gain portfolio experience, and ship real products that matter.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Sign Up

    Create a free account and tell us about your experience level and interests.

  2. 2

    Get Matched

    We pair you with a mentor and a cohort of developers working on similar goals.

  3. 3

    Contribute

    Join a project, attend events, and build your skills week by week.

  4. 4

    Level Up
